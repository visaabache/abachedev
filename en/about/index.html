About

An independent web studio, based in Morocco.

AbacheDev designs and builds fast, mobile-friendly websites made to be found on Google, for small businesses, shops, freelancers and startups in Morocco and worldwide.

My mission

To give every business a website it’s proud of and that brings in customers: clear, fast, easy to find on Google and simple to update. No jargon, no hidden fees: a clear quote, agreed with you before any work starts.

What makes the difference

  • One point of contact, from the first chat to launch, reachable directly on WhatsApp or by email.
  • Fixed, published prices: Starter 2,990 MAD (about $299), Business 6,990 MAD (about $699), E-commerce 12,990 MAD (about $1,299).
  • Mobile first: most of your customers find you on their phone, so your site is designed for them.
  • SEO included in every plan, not sold as an extra.
  • Launch in 1 to 3 weeks depending on the project, and a reply within 24 hours.

How I work

  1. Discovery. We talk about your business, your goals and the style you like. You get a clear quote and a timeline.
  2. Design. I create a mockup for you to approve, and we refine it together until it feels like you.
  3. Build. Your site is built, tested on every kind of device and optimized for speed and SEO.
  4. Launch. The site goes live on your domain. I show you how to update your content and stay available to help.

Three languages, in Morocco and beyond

I work in French, English and Arabic, with clients in Morocco and around the world. Everything can be done remotely: WhatsApp, email and video calls.
