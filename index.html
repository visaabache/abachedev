FAQ

Web design FAQ

How much does a website cost?

A one-page Starter website costs $299, a Business website of up to 5 pages costs $699, and an online store starts at $1,299. Every quote is fixed and agreed before work begins.

Will my website show up on Google?

Yes. Every website includes on-page SEO: fast loading, mobile-friendly design, meta tags, structured data and a sitemap submitted to Google Search Console, so Google can find and index your pages.

Do you work with clients outside Morocco?

Yes. I work with businesses in Morocco and around the world. Everything can be handled online over WhatsApp, email and video calls.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites take 1–3 weeks depending on size. A one-page Starter site can be ready in about 5 days.

Do I need to provide the content and images?

It helps if you have your logo, text and photos, but I can help write copy and source quality stock images too.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. I'll set things up so you can edit text and images easily, and show you how in a short walkthrough.

Do you help with domain and hosting?

Absolutely. I'll help you pick and connect a domain and set up reliable, affordable hosting.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to start, and the remaining 50% when you're happy and ready to launch.
